OTT Streaming Content Trend Analysis 🎬
📌 Project Overview
This project explores OTT streaming content from platforms such as Netflix and Amazon Prime Video to understand content distribution, genre popularity, release-year trends, content ratings, and regional representation.

The goal is to transform raw catalog data into meaningful insights that can support content strategy and business decision-making.

🎯 Objectives
Analyze movie and TV show distribution.
Identify the most common genres.
Explore content release-year trends.
Analyze content ratings.
Examine country-wise content distribution.
Compare content catalogs across streaming platforms.
🛠️ Tools & Technologies
Python: Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn
SQL: Filtering, aggregations, GROUP BY, and joins
Excel: Data exploration and reporting
Jupyter Notebook: Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

💡 Business Value
The analysis can help identify catalog patterns, understand content variety, and explore potential opportunities for content acquisition and regional programming.

Catalog data alone does not measure audience demand; viewing hours, subscriber retention, and other audience metrics would be needed to validate business recommendations.
